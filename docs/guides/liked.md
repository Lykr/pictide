# Liked

[简体中文](../zh-CN/guides/liked.md)

[Documentation](../README.md) · [Roam](roam.md) / [Generate](generate.md)

Liked gathers your favorites from Roam and Generate. Revisit images, find inspiration, and help Pictide learn what you enjoy.

## Browse and manage likes

Click an image to view it in full; see [Image browsing](images.md) for more. You can remove a like when an image no longer appeals to you.

Liked images stay in your collection, even when you [clear generation results](generate.md#clear-generation-results).

To continue exploring from a favorite, open it and choose **Roam from here**, the compass immediately left of the immersive-mode button. Roam opens on that original, paused, with its positive tags pinned except for terms already fixed by the current preset. These replace your old pins; old exclusions and the pinned canvas are cleared. Your current preset and other Roam settings remain in use. Old future prefetch is discarded; the image stays liked and keeps its original source. This also works for favorites already hidden from Roam or Generate.

## Save and export images

Open a liked image and choose **Download original** to save its original file. Choose the **Select to export** icon immediately to the right of the page's blur button, select images, and choose **Export ZIP**. Click the icon again to exit selection. **Select all loaded** selects the images currently loaded in the list. Load more first to include older images. Each export supports up to 1,000 images.

Removing a like starts a 24-hour grace period before automatic age or Roam-count cleanup can delete the image. After the grace period, the ordinary [cleanup rules](settings.md#historical-image-cleanup) apply.

## Recover a removed like

Open **Recently unliked**, the history icon to the right of the export button. It shows images still in their 24-hour grace period, with the remaining time and a **Like again** action. The list survives refreshes and restarts and includes images already hidden from Roam or Generate. Restoring an image returns it to Liked without changing those pages' visibility.

The recovery dialog shares Liked's blur setting. Open an image to view it in full; restore its like before downloading the original. Closing the dialog preserves your collection's filters, sort, and scroll position. After the grace period the image leaves this list and normal cleanup rules apply.

## Preference score sorting

Choose **Newest** to find recent favorites, or **Preference score** to see images closer to your current taste first. After giving more feedback, refresh scores manually to update the order.

## Tag filters

Search and select tags to narrow your collection. Selecting several tags shows images that contain all of them.
